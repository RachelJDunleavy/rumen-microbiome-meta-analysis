
# Rumen Microbiome and Methane Mitigation Meta-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project investigates the effects of methane mitigation strategies on the cattle rumen microbiome through a multi-study meta-analysis of publicly available microbiome sequencing datasets.

The research integrates bioinformatics, microbial ecology, and statistical modeling to evaluate changes in microbial diversity and community composition associated with methane mitigation.

## Research Objectives

1. Evaluate the effects of methane mitigation strategies on rumen microbial alpha diversity.
2. Assess changes in microbial community composition and beta diversity.
3. Compare microbiome responses across methane mitigation strategies.
4. Quantify treatment effects through statistical modeling and meta-analysis.

## Methane Mitigation Strategies

- 3-Nitrooxypropanol (3-NOP)
- Seaweeds and algae
- Plant secondary compounds
- Oils and lipids
- Ionophores and antibiotics
- Biochar
- Dietary formulation

## Bioinformatics and Statistical Methods

- QIIME 2
- 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing
- Metadata harmonization
- Alpha and beta diversity analysis
- Principal coordinates analysis (PCoA)
- PERMANOVA
- Mixed-effects modeling
- Meta-analysis and effect size estimation
